Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-21

Folio helps you create invoices and estimates, track payments and run a small business from your iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have. “We” means the developer of Folio. “You” means the person using the app.

Short version: your data is yours. We don’t sell it, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your iPhone

Everything you create in Folio — your business details, logo, clients, items, invoices, estimates, payments, expenses, time entries, signatures, attachments and settings — is saved in a database on your iPhone first. The app works without an account and without a connection.

If you never sign in, this information stays on your iPhone and we never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Backup and sync (when you sign in)

If you sign in with Apple, Google or email, your data is backed up to our servers so you can restore it on a new phone and share it with your team. We store it with Supabase, our database and hosting provider, in its Asia Pacific (Mumbai, India) region.

We keep your email address and sign-in method to identify your account. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our provider, and each business’s data is only readable by its own members.

AI features

Voice to invoice, AI estimates, receipt scanning, the logo maker and the assistant only run when you use them. For each request we send what the feature needs — for example your voice recording, a photo of a receipt, your message, or item names and prices — to OpenAI through our servers.

OpenAI processes it to give you the answer. Under OpenAI’s API terms it isn’t used to train their models and may be kept for up to 30 days to prevent abuse. You can turn AI features off at any time in Settings → AI & privacy.

Email and web invoice links

When you send a document by email or link, we create a private web page for it and send the email through Resend, our email delivery provider. This uses your client’s name and email address, the document and your business details.

When your client opens the link, we record that it was opened so we can tell you. We don’t use cookies for advertising on these pages.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction details and an app user ID linked to your account, not your name or card.

Your clients’ information

When you add clients, you decide what to store and you are responsible for having a right to use it. We process it only to provide Folio to you — to save, back up, print and send your documents — and never contact your clients for our own purposes.

Diagnostics and support

If you write to support and choose to include app details, we receive the app version, iPhone model and iOS version. We don’t include your invoices or clients. We may keep crash information that doesn’t identify you to fix bugs.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent your information. We don’t use it for advertising. We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. Items you delete go to Trash for 30 days and are then deleted. When you delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account, we delete your account and the businesses you own; copies in backups are removed within 30 days.

Where it is processed

Our providers may process data in India, the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your information, or object to how we use it. You can export your data in the app and delete your account yourself. For anything else, email us and we’ll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Folio is a business tool and isn’t meant for anyone under 16. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above. If a change is significant, we’ll tell you in the app before it applies.
