Delete your account

Removes your account and everything backed up with it

In the app

Open Folio → Settings → Account → Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account, businesses, clients, documents, files and backups are deleted from our servers and from the iPhone.

Without the app

Email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We’ll confirm within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
