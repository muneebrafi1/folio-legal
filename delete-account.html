Delete your account

Removes your account and everything backed up with it

In the app

Open Folio → Settings → Account → Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account and the businesses you own — clients, documents, files, web links and AI history — are deleted from our servers and from the iPhone. This also works if you never signed in: Folio backs up to a private account from the moment you set it up.

Deleting the app isn’t enough

Removing the app from your iPhone deletes the copy on the phone, not the backup on our servers. Use Delete account first.

Without the app

Email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We’ll confirm within 30 days. A private account you never signed in to has no email address, so it can only be deleted in the app.

What we keep

Only what we need for accounting and to prevent abuse, without your name or email: records of subscription purchases, counts of AI requests and error-log entries with your account ID removed. Copies in our provider’s automatic backups expire within 30 days. Details in the privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
