Privacy policy

Folio helps you create invoices and estimates, track payments and run a small business from your iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, who helps us process it and the choices you have. “We” means the developer of Folio. “You” means the person using the app.

Short version: from the moment you set up the app, your business data is backed up to our servers, even if you never sign in. We use it only to run the app for you. We don’t sell it, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

01 · Your private account and backup

You don’t need to sign up. When you finish setting up your business, the app creates a private account for you in the background — no name, email or password — and starts backing up what you create in Folio to our servers. If you’re offline, the backup starts when you’re back online.

Until you sign in, only this iPhone can open that account: its key is kept in this iPhone’s Keychain. If you lose the iPhone before you sign in, we may not be able to give your data back.

If you sign in (for example with Sign in with Apple), your backup becomes part of that account, so you can restore it on a new phone and invite your team. We then also keep your email address (with Sign in with Apple, the one Apple gives us, which can be a private relay address) and how you sign in.

Deleting the app removes the copy on your iPhone, not the backup on our servers. To delete the backup, use Delete account (see “Deleting your data”).

02 · What we store

Your business details and logo; your clients’ details (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses); items and prices; invoices, estimates, payments, expenses, time entries, recurring invoices and late fees; signatures, attachments (photos and PDFs) and receipt photos; your payment details (such as bank details or payment links), because they are printed on your invoices; and your settings.

Technical details: a random ID for each install of the app (so two phones on one account stay in sync), the app version and, if you allow notifications, your iPhone’s push token.

The PDFs of your invoices and estimates are made on your iPhone and aren’t uploaded. When you share a document as a web link, we also store a copy of how it looks, to show on the web page.

03 · Where it is stored and how it is protected

We store your data with Supabase, our database and hosting provider, in its Asia Pacific (Mumbai, India) region. A copy also stays in the app’s database on your iPhone, so the app works offline.

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our provider. Each business’s data can only be read by that business’s own members, according to their role.

04 · AI features (only with your permission)

AI features — voice to invoice, AI estimates, receipt scanning, the logo and image maker and the assistant — only run after you allow them. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → AI & privacy.

When you use one, we send what that feature needs to OpenAI through our servers: for example your voice recording, a photo, a file you attach, your message, item names and prices, and your business’s name, industry, country and currency so the answer fits your business. OpenAI processes it to give you the answer. Under OpenAI’s API terms it isn’t used to train their models, and OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse.

On our servers we keep the recordings, photos and files you upload for AI, the images, logos and estimate drafts it makes, and your assistant conversations (so it can follow the conversation). We also count your AI requests to apply the daily limits — for accounts without a subscription, also per network, using a scrambled (hashed) form of the IP address.

05 · Web invoice links

When you share a document as a link, we create a private web page for it with a long random address. The page is served by Cloudflare and the document comes from our servers. Your client sees the document, your business details and logo, and the payment details and QR code you chose to show.

Anyone who has the link can open it, so send it only to your client. Search engines are asked not to list these pages. You can reset a link (the old one stops working), and it stops working when you delete or void the document.

On the page your client can accept an estimate (by typing their name or drawing a signature), decline it with a reason, or tell you they’ve paid with a short note. We save those answers with your document. We record when the page is opened, not counting bots and link previews, so we can tell you.

To stop abuse we use a scrambled (hashed) form of the visitor’s IP address for rate limits; we don’t store the IP address itself. The page uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

06 · Email (not switched on yet)

Sending documents, statements and reminders by email from the app isn’t switched on yet. Today you send a PDF or a link yourself — for example from Messages or your own Mail app — and we don’t see those messages.

When we switch it on, emails will go through Resend, our email delivery provider, with your client’s name and email address, your message, the document and your business details. We’ll keep a record of each email (who it went to, when, and whether it was delivered or bounced) and limit how many emails an account can send. We’ll update this policy before it starts.

07 · Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your iPhone’s push token. Alerts such as “an invoice was viewed” are sent through Expo’s push service to Apple’s push service, so their text — which can include a client’s name and a document number — passes through them.

Reminders the app schedules itself, such as “an invoice is overdue”, are created on your iPhone and don’t go through our servers.

08 · Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase details, your account ID (not your name or email) and basic technical details such as the app version and your App Store country. We keep your subscription status and the purchase events RevenueCat sends us.

09 · Your clients’ information

When you add clients, you decide what to store and you are responsible for having a right to use it: for your clients’ data you are in charge (the “controller”) and we process it for you. We use it only to provide Folio to you — to save, back up, print, share and send your documents — and we never contact your clients for our own purposes.

When you add a client from your iPhone’s contacts, only the contact you pick is copied. Your address book is never uploaded.

10 · Diagnostics and support

The app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

Our servers keep an error log to find problems, for example a failed email or a total that didn’t match. An entry can include your account ID, a document’s ID and a short technical message, but not the contents of your documents. Our providers also keep short-lived technical logs of requests (such as IP address and time) to run and protect the service.

When you email us from the app, the draft includes the app version, iPhone model and iOS version; you can remove them before you send. If you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may share crash reports with us under Apple’s terms.

11 · Who helps us

Supabase: database, file storage and server functions (Mumbai, India). OpenAI: AI features, only when you’ve allowed them. RevenueCat: subscription status. Apple: App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple and push notifications. Expo: passes notifications on to Apple. Cloudflare: serves the web invoice page. Resend: email delivery, once email from the app is switched on.

They process your data only to run these services for us. We don’t sell or rent your information, we don’t use it for advertising, we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

12 · How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists — including a private account you never signed in to.

Items you delete go to Trash, which shows the date each one was deleted. Automatic emptying after 30 days isn’t running yet: until it is, items stay in Trash until you choose Delete forever. Delete forever erases the item’s content, and any safety copies of it, from our servers straight away; a small marker (its ID, a document’s number so it’s never reused, and for a recurring invoice which schedule made it) stays so your other phones remove it too.

Some things are meant to be removed by a scheduled clean-up job that isn’t running yet: files you delete (such as photos and attachments) and the files of a business you delete, what you upload for AI, and older safety copies of documents you still have. Until it runs, they stay in private storage on our servers. Deleting your account removes them, except the files of a business you had already deleted, which wait for the clean-up job. Email us if you want them removed sooner.

When you delete your account, your data is deleted straight away (see “Deleting your data”), and copies in our provider’s automatic backups expire within 30 days. We keep only what we need for accounting and to prevent abuse, without your name or email: records of subscription purchases, counts of AI requests, and error-log entries with your account ID removed.

13 · Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. We delete your account and the businesses you own — their clients, documents, files, web links and backups — plus your AI uploads, images and conversations and your push tokens, then remove that data from this iPhone. If you’re a member of someone else’s business, you leave it; what you created there stays with that business. If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also remove the app in your Apple Account settings under Sign in with Apple.

Without the app: email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we’ll delete your account and confirm within 30 days. A private account you never signed in to can only be deleted in the app, because we can’t tell which one is yours.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings.

14 · Where it is processed

Your data is stored in India. Our other providers may process it in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses in our providers’ data processing terms.

15 · Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your information, or object to how we use it. You can delete your account yourself, and with a subscription export your data in the app (Tools → Data). Anyone can email us for a copy of their data or for anything else; we’ll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

16 · Children

Folio is a business tool for adults. It isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. Where the law sets a higher age (up to 16 in parts of the EU), you must be at least that age to use it. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.
